Four decades of compassionate kidney care

Experienced nephrologists delivering comprehensive, personalized care for Oklahoma families since 1984.

Nephrology Specialists of Oklahoma provide comprehensive care for individuals with kidney-related issues — led by experienced nephrologists who emphasize the importance of healthy kidneys for overall well-being.

We diagnose, treat, and manage a wide range of kidney disorders including chronic kidney disease, kidney stones, renal hypertension, glomerular diseases, and electrolyte imbalances. Our practice combines advanced medical technology with personalized attention to meet each patient's unique needs.

We adopt a holistic approach to kidney health, considering both medical factors and lifestyle and dietary influences on kidney function. Patient education is a key focus — our providers take the time to explain diagnoses, treatment options, and lifestyle adjustments to build a strong patient–doctor partnership.

We collaborate closely with other medical specialties to provide comprehensive care, especially for patients with complex conditions affecting kidney health. Our goal is to create a comfortable, welcoming environment where patients can openly discuss their health concerns.
