Kidney Clinic

Comprehensive general nephrology for chronic kidney disease, hypertension, and electrolyte disorders.

Expert kidney care in Tulsa

At our nephrology outpatient clinic, we provide specialized, comprehensive care for all types of kidney-related conditions — including chronic kidney disease (CKD), electrolyte imbalances, hypertension, glomerular diseases, and more. Our experienced nephrologists offer expert evaluation, personalized treatment plans, and continuous monitoring in a supportive, office-based setting.

Patient-focused CKD management & education

Chronic kidney disease is a progressive condition that can lead to serious complications if not properly managed. We focus on early detection, patient education, and proactive care to slow progression and improve long-term outcomes. Our team works closely with registered dietitians, endocrinologists, vascular surgeons, and other specialists for a truly multidisciplinary approach — including dialysis education and preparation for a seamless transition when necessary.

Our mission

We believe informed patients are empowered patients. Whether you are newly diagnosed or managing advanced kidney disease, our goal is to help you live a healthier, more informed life. We coordinate closely with primary care physicians for a smooth referral process and continuity of care.

If you or a loved one is experiencing signs of kidney disease — such as swelling, fatigue, or abnormal lab results — schedule an appointment today for expert outpatient kidney care you can trust.
