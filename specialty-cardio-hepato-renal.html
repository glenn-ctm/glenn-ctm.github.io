Heart & Liver Related Kidney Disease

Specialized kidney care for patients with advanced heart failure and liver disease.

Specialized kidney care for complex conditions

When the heart or liver is severely diseased, the kidneys are often affected due to reduced blood flow, fluid imbalance, and hormonal changes. Our clinic provides focused kidney care for patients with advanced heart failure and cirrhosis.

Why kidney protection matters in heart failure and cirrhosis

Patients with these conditions are at high risk of:

  • Acute kidney injury (AKI)
  • Worsening chronic kidney disease (CKD)
  • Fluid overload and hospitalization
  • Complications requiring dialysis

Our approach

  • Comprehensive evaluation of patients with advanced heart and liver disease.
  • Individualized, kidney-focused care plans.
  • Close collaboration with cardiologists, hepatologists, and transplant teams.
  • Ongoing monitoring with CardioMEMS™ and other advanced tools.

Why choose our advanced nephrology clinic?

  • Expertise in managing kidney health in heart failure and cirrhosis.
  • Use of innovative tools like CardioMEMS™ for optimal outcomes.
  • A multidisciplinary approach integrating nephrology, cardiology, and hepatology.
  • A focus on preventing hospitalizations and preserving kidney function.
