Outreach Clinics

Specialty kidney care delivered closer to home for rural and tribal Oklahoma communities.

Expanding access to nephrology care beyond Tulsa

At Nephrology Specialists of Oklahoma, we're committed to meeting our patients where they are — both in their health journey and in their communities. That's why we've expanded our services beyond Tulsa, offering clinics in peripheral locations to make specialized kidney care more accessible and convenient.

Many patients living with chronic kidney disease, electrolyte imbalances, high blood pressure, and other kidney-related conditions face challenges traveling for care. By providing clinics outside Tulsa, we reduce the burden of long-distance travel and ensure patients receive consistent, high-quality care close to home.

Partnering with the Muscogee Nation

We're proud to collaborate with the Muscogee Nation Department of Health to expand access to high-quality nephrology care for citizens of the Muscogee Nation. Through this partnership, we provide specialized kidney care closer to home — ensuring timely diagnosis, treatment, and follow-up for chronic kidney disease and related conditions. Together, we deliver compassionate, culturally respectful care to the Muscogee community.

These regional clinics reflect our commitment to patient-centered care, improving follow-up rates, treatment adherence, and overall outcomes. We continue to expand into new locations over time.
