What To Expect

Your first visit, simplified.

Our team of nephrologists, advanced practitioners, and nurses will guide you through every step of your kidney care journey — starting from day one.

  • Complete medical history and physical examination
  • Review of your current and past lab results
  • Medication review with adjustments as needed
  • Personalized care plan shared with your primary physician
